## 3. Project Description Draft (~300 words)

> **TerraTwin: Screening Terrestrial Analogues for Jezero Crater**

**Overview**  
Planetary exploration missions rehearse surface operations, test rover navigation systems, and evaluate autonomy software on Earth before flight. TerraTwin is an open-source screening tool that identifies 1° terrestrial analogue candidate regions for Mars's Jezero Crater across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA).

**Methodology & Data Sources**  
TerraTwin evaluates 1,960 land cells (1° × 1° resolution, approx. 111 km) across North Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. The pipeline calculates multi-criteria similarity against Jezero Crater target values derived from the Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) and NASA POWER reanalysis datasets. Screening criteria encompass terrain geometry (elevation relief and p90 slope percentiles from AWS terrain tiles) and surface environmental conditions (annual solar irradiance, temperature range, and aridity index). Operation suitability layers assess rover transit feasibility based on terrain steepness thresholds.

**Honest Scientific Findings & Limitations**  
TerraTwin delivers exploratory screening hypotheses, not confirmed geological discoveries. In our preliminary validation test against 4 published analogue candidates and 2 negative controls (n=6), terrain geometry alone did not cleanly separate known analogues from control sites (ROC-AUC 0.50). Only the Western Desert inverted channels ranked in the top 5% overall (95.0 percentile); other sites scored modestly. Mineralogical and compositional data are not included in this phase. Regional 1° cells omit sub-kilometer obstacles, boulders, and local rover-scale hazards.

**Interactive Platform & AI Transparency**  
The web interface features an interactive 2D GIS map, 3D globe and terrain elevation block inspection, an arcade driving simulation, and WebXR virtual reality viewing. The application requires no login and operates without server backends or trackers. We used Claude (Anthropic) for prompt specification and method design, and the Antigravity coding agent for data pipeline execution and frontend development under human oversight. No synthetic observations or scientific scores were generated by AI.
